How Echo matches people

  1. 01

    Import

    Echo reads your top 50 artists from the last month, six months and all time, and weights long-term taste most.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Your taste becomes a vector of artists. Sharing a niche artist counts for far more than sharing a superstar.

  3. 03

    Discover

    People rank higher when the artists they love fit your taste and are new to you. It has to work both ways.

  4. 04

    Connect

    Matches stay anonymous until you both choose to connect. Then you can start trading songs.
